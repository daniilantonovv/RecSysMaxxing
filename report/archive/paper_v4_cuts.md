# What the short paper v4 leaves out

On 17 October 2026 `report/paper_v4.typ` was cut from 33 to 13 pages. On 18 October it was revised after a referee's
critique and now has 15: 10 pages of main text, 1 of references (two columns, small type) and 4 of appendix. No
result changed in either step. The long version is commit `839539d`:

```bash
git show 839539d:report/paper_v4.typ > paper_v4_long.typ
```

Every removed table is still generated by `report/tables.py` into `report/tables/<name>.typ`. It goes back into the
paper with `#report-table("<name>", [caption]) <label>`. Rebuilding the long version exactly also needs
`git show 839539d:report/tables.py`, because five generated tables have changed since:

- `ranking_metrics`: the HitRate panel is gone (NDCG, Recall, MAP at K = 1, 5, 10 remain).
- `concentration`: rows β = 1 and β = 0.3 (KION) and β = 0.3 and β = 0.1 (Yambda) are gone.
- `long_term_rewards`: rows KION "discount 0, β = 0.1", Yambda "return after the list, β = 0.1" and both Yambda
  discount-0 rows are gone.
- `seeds`: rows added for "KL at β = 0.3, value model", the validation-selected β = 1 on KION, re-ranking by the
  relative share against the ranked lists, and the base recommender's predicted share, logged share and surplus.
- `history_length`, `titles`: labels only. Quartiles read "5–8 events" instead of "5 to 9 events", and "ДУХLESS" reads
  "Dukhless".

## Text

- **Abstract:** the numbers per question except the trade-off, and the keywords.
- **Introduction:** the list of five contributions (Table 1, the abstract and the related-work paragraph carry them).
- **Related Work:** the first paragraph is shorter, and a positioning paragraph was added. No longer cited:
  bengio2015scheduled, botta2025pinrec, christakopoulou2022reward, coste2023ensembles, deffayet2023offline,
  deffayet2023slate, dudik2011dr, ferraridacrema2019progress, gao2022cirs, guo2026promise, janner2019mbpo,
  jaques2016sequencetutor, loshchilov2017adamw, mann2018delayed, ranzato2015sequence, schulman2015trpo,
  shi2018virtualtaobao, su2023exploration, williams1992reinforce, wu2019brac, xin2021sa2c.
- **Method:** subsections became paragraphs, and the list of instruments became one paragraph. Gone:
  - the relative-share formula (mid-rank quantile) and the ten buckets of the previous share;
  - the choice head and why no reward uses it;
  - the rule that a retention outcome counts only if its waiting time lies inside the data;
  - how likes are attributed (latest play within a day) and the reaction heads' loss weight;
  - the hidden-response models' validation loss;
  - the state, return and policy-gradient formulas, and the value model's architecture;
  - the KL-budget update rule (β ← β·exp(η(KL/δ − 1)), η = 0.03);
  - best of n lists;
  - details of liked-only fine-tuning and MMR.
- **Setup:**
  - the baselines paragraph (popularity, replay, Markov chain, GRU4Rec, SASRec, BERT4Rec, untuned);
  - hyperparameters: AdamW, λ = 0.9, clip 0.2, value-model learning rate 1e-4 with 50 warm-up iterations,
    leave-one-out 4 × 128 lists;
  - the bootstrap description, "sweeps are reported in full", and the review and seed session times;
  - that the review sessions reproduced the base recommenders exactly.

  Greedy decoding, how KL is measured, how training histories are drawn, the filters, the session and return
  definitions, and that replay reads the same window are all in the text again since 18 October.
- **Results:**
  - The seed-stability paragraph: β = 0.1 on KION varies most (NDCG@10 0.079 ± 0.012); the Yambda personal share is
    16% ± 16% (now in Q1); γ = 0 is ahead of full credit in every seed (lowest 0.1039 against highest 0.1037);
    one-item episodes overlap.
  - Yambda base-model details: the recommender is best on new tracks (0.032), and popularity reaches a third of it.
  - Q1: Yambda popularity shift +13%; the value model explains 78% (Yambda) against 49% (KION) of the variance of
    returns.
  - Q2: Yambda budgets 0.2 and 0.5 end at nearly the same weight (0.066 / 0.067). The Yambda ranking-metric range is
    now "within 1.3%" because HitRate (+2.2%) is gone.
  - Q5:
    - the cost per unit of share from each method's own start (re-ranking −7.6% NDCG@10 for +0.08, RL −10.7% for
      +0.10);
    - Yambda off-policy REINFORCE +6% NDCG@10 (still in the seeds table: 0.1256 ± 0.0009);
    - Yambda re-ranking less relevant than RL (0.1155 vs 0.1190, also in the seeds table).

- **Online experiment:** the success criteria ("B is better than A if the consumed share of served recommendations

- **Limitations:** eleven bullets became one paragraph. Gone:
  - that the list reward's distance is measured in the base recommender's own embedding space;
  - the details of which Yambda grids are narrower.

  predicted for RL's lists is above every group, so it is an extrapolation both reward models make alike.
